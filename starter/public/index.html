IPL AUCTION RULES & GUIDELINES

1. CUSTOM FRANCHISE BUDGET

Every team begins with the host-configured budget (e.g. ₹100.00 Cr to ₹150.00 Cr). Bids are automatically deducted from the winning team's purse upon player sale.

2. CUSTOM FOREIGN PLAYER LIMIT

Each squad is restricted to the configured foreign player limit (e.g. 4 foreign players). Once all foreign slots are filled, the system prevents the franchise from placing bids on foreign players.

3. CUSTOM MAXIMUM SQUAD CAPACITY

Each franchise can purchase up to the configured maximum squad capacity (e.g. 25 players). Teams that fill their squad are blocked from further bidding.

4. 10-SECOND AUCTION TIMER

Each player starts with a 10-second countdown. Every valid bid immediately resets the countdown back to 10 seconds. When the timer hits 0s, the player is sold to the highest bidder.

5. BID INCREMENTS

Bids below ₹5.00 Cr increase by ₹0.25 Cr. Bids between ₹5.00 Cr and ₹10.00 Cr increase by ₹0.50 Cr. Bids exceeding ₹10.00 Cr increase by ₹1.00 Cr.
