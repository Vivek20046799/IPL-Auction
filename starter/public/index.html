IPL AUCTION OFFICIAL RULES & REGULATIONS

1. FRANCHISE PURSE

Every team begins with a solid purse of ₹100.00 Crore. Bids are automatically deducted from the winning team's purse upon player sale.

2. OVERSEAS PLAYER RESTRICTION

Each squad can acquire a maximum of 4 Overseas players. Once 4 overseas slots are filled, the system blocks the team from bidding on further overseas players.

3. 10-SECOND AUCTION TIMER

Each player starts with a 10-second countdown. Every valid bid immediately resets the countdown back to 10 seconds. When the timer hits 0s, the player is sold to the highest bidder or marked unsold if no bids were received.

4. BID INCREMENTS

Bids below ₹5.00 Cr increase by ₹0.25 Cr. Bids between ₹5.00 Cr and ₹10.00 Cr increase by ₹0.50 Cr. Bids exceeding ₹10.00 Cr increase by ₹1.00 Cr.

5. EQUAL PARTICIPATION

The host creates and manages the room, but bids as an equal participant under identical purse and overseas limitations.
